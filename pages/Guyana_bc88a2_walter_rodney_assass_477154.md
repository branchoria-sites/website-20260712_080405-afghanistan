## Introduction

The assassination of Walter Rodney is no longer a mystery in the sense of not knowing that he was deliberately killed. The central unresolved question today is different: who authorised the operation, how far responsibility extended within the Guyanese state, and why no one has ever been held criminally accountable. Rodney, the internationally respected historian and leading figure in the Working People's Alliance (WPA), died in an explosion in Georgetown on 13 June 1980 after receiving what he believed was a communications device from [Guyana]({{ 'guyana/' | relative_url }}) Defence Force sergeant Gregory Smith. Decades of investigations, testimony and documentary evidence have transformed understanding of the case, yet the absence of a criminal trial means that many aspects of command responsibility remain legally untested.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORTOFTHE COMMISSION OF INQUIRYAPPOINTED TO ENQUIRE AND REPORT ON THE CIRCUMSTANCESMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

## What Is Still Unknown About Rodney's Assassination?

Walter Rodney's death occurred during one of the most politically tense periods in modern Guyanese history. As a historian, trade union supporter and opposition activist, Rodney had become one of the most prominent critics of the government led by Prime Minister Forbes Burnham. His movements were monitored, opposition groups reported harassment, and relations between the state and its critics had become increasingly confrontational.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORTOFTHE COMMISSION OF INQUIRYAPPOINTED TO ENQUIRE AND REPORT ON THE CIRCUMSTANCESMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

What remains disputed is not whether Rodney died in a deliberate attack but the full chain of responsibility. Historians and investigators distinguish between three separate questions:

* **Who physically delivered the fatal device?** The evidence overwhelmingly points to Gregory Smith.
* **Who organised or authorised the operation?** This remains the central unresolved issue because no criminal court has examined the evidence.
* **Why has accountability remained incomplete?** The passage of time, the deaths of key figures, missing opportunities for prosecution and political disagreement have all contributed to the lack of judicial resolution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORTOFTHE COMMISSION OF INQUIRYAPPOINTED TO ENQUIRE AND REPORT ON THE CIRCUMSTANCESMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

## The Explosion and the Competing Early Accounts

On the evening of 13 June 1980, Walter Rodney and his brother Donald Rodney met Gregory Smith, a serving sergeant in the Guyana Defence Force. Smith supplied Rodney with what appeared to be a walkie-talkie. Shortly afterwards, while Walter Rodney sat in a parked car in Georgetown and Donald Rodney stood nearby, the device exploded. Walter Rodney was killed instantly, while Donald Rodney survived with injuries and later became the principal eyewitness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-title">5851 report of the commission of inquiry appointed to enquire and report on the circumstances surrounding the death in an explosion of th</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE COMMISSION OF INQUIRY -• I • •October 19, 2025</span></span></span>

Almost immediately, competing explanations emerged.

The government's early position suggested that Rodney had accidentally killed himself while handling explosives connected with an intended act of sabotage. Supporters of Rodney and members of the WPA rejected this account, arguing that he had been deceived into accepting an explosive device disguised as radio equipment. The competing narratives reflected the intensely polarised political climate of the time and shaped public debate for decades.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.walterrodneyfoundation.org/news-media" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: walterrodneyfoundation.org">[The Walter Rodney Foundation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">walterrodneyfoundation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Walter Rodney Foundation New &amp; Media — The Walter Rodney Foundation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Walter Rodney Foundation New &amp; Media — The Walter Rodney Foundation</span></span></span>

Subsequent evidence steadily weakened the official version. Donald Rodney consistently maintained that his brother believed the object was a communications device rather than a bomb. The later Commission of Inquiry accepted this account, finding no persuasive evidence that Walter Rodney expected the device to explode or intended to use it for terrorist purposes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-title">5851 report of the commission of inquiry appointed to enquire and report on the circumstances surrounding the death in an explosion of th</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE COMMISSION OF INQUIRY -• I • •October 19, 2025</span></span></span>

## Gregory Smith and the State-Linked Operation

Gregory Smith occupies the central position in the evidence because he was the last known person to supply Rodney with the device.

Smith served in the Guyana Defence Force under the alias "Cyril Johnson" after leaving Guyana. Following Rodney's death, he rapidly departed the country and settled in French Guiana, where he remained beyond the reach of Guyanese investigators until his death in 2002. His successful escape became one of the strongest reasons many observers suspected official assistance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stabroeknews.com/2016/02/20/news/guyana/rodney-victim-state-organised-killing-pm-burnham-known-coi-finds/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stabroeknews.com">[Stabroek News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stabroeknews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stabroek NewsRodney was victim of state-organised killing, PM Burnham had to have known –CoI finds - Stabroek News...</span></span></span>

Smith later denied intentionally murdering Rodney and promoted an alternative version of events through a memoir published with his sister after his death. The Commission of Inquiry examined these claims in detail and concluded that they were inconsistent with the witness testimony and documentary evidence presented during the hearings. It specifically rejected suggestions that Rodney's own political colleagues had engineered his death.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORTOFTHE COMMISSION OF INQUIRYAPPOINTED TO ENQUIRE AND REPORT ON THE CIRCUMSTANCESMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

The Commission instead concluded that:

* Gregory Smith knowingly supplied the explosive device.
* The device had been disguised as a communications radio.
* Smith did not act entirely alone.
* Elements within state agencies encouraged, assisted or protected him.
* His departure from Guyana required assistance that the Commission considered beyond the capability of Rodney's political allies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-title">5851 report of the commission of inquiry appointed to enquire and report on the circumstances surrounding the death in an explosion of th</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE COMMISSION OF INQUIRY -• I • •October 19, 2025</span></span></span>

These findings substantially altered the historical understanding of the case by shifting attention from the individual assassin to the possibility of organised state involvement.

## Why the Inquiry Changed the Historical Record

For many years, no comprehensive public investigation revisited the evidence. That changed when a Commission of Inquiry was established in 2014, more than three decades after Rodney's death.

The inquiry heard evidence from dozens of witnesses, reviewed earlier investigations and examined documentary material that had never previously been considered together. Its report, released in 2016, concluded that Rodney had been assassinated and that Gregory Smith acted as an agent of the state, aided and encouraged by members of state institutions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.walterrodneyfoundation.org/coi/report-the-commission-of-inquiry-on-the-death-of-walter-rodney-february-2016" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: walterrodneyfoundation.org">[The Walter Rodney Foundation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">walterrodneyfoundation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on walterrodneyfoundation.org.</span></span></span>

The Commission went further than previous official examinations. On the balance of probabilities—the civil standard used by commissions rather than criminal courts—it concluded that the killing formed part of a state-organised operation and that such an operation could not realistically have occurred without the knowledge of Prime Minister Forbes Burnham, given the highly centralised political system of the period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/pdf/reprt-of-the-commission-of-inquiry.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nsarchive.gwu.edu">[National Security Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nsarchive.gwu.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Security Archive REPORT</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OF THE COMMISSION OF INQUIRY APPOINT...</span></span></span>

These findings are historically significant, but they should also be understood within their legal limits. A commission of inquiry is not a criminal court. Its conclusions do not amount to criminal convictions and were not tested through cross-examination under the rules applicable to a criminal prosecution.

## Why Judicial Accountability Remains Incomplete

The central mystery today concerns accountability rather than causation.

Several factors have prevented the case from reaching a definitive judicial conclusion:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **The principal alleged perpetrator died before trial.** Gregory Smith died in French Guiana in 2002 without facing prosecution.
* **Many senior officials connected with the period also died before the inquiry reported.**
* **The Commission's findings have never been translated into criminal proceedings establishing responsibility beyond reasonable doubt.**
* **Political disagreement over the inquiry's conclusions has continued, reflecting Guyana's long-standing partisan divisions.**<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORTOFTHE COMMISSION OF INQUIRYAPPOINTED TO ENQUIRE AND REPORT ON THE CIRCUMSTANCESMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

</div>

As a result, there remains a distinction between historical judgement and legal accountability. Historians generally treat the Commission as the fullest official reconstruction of the assassination, while recognising that no court has determined individual criminal liability for those alleged to have organised or facilitated the operation.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Guyana_bc88a2_walter_rodney_assass_477154-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Walter Rodney illustration 3" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Guyana_bc88a2_walter_rodney_assass_477154-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Guyana_bc88a2_walter_rodney_assass_477154-Illustration-3-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">

## Why the Case Still Matters

Walter Rodney's assassination remains one of Guyana's defining unresolved historical cases because it combines well-established facts with enduring uncertainty about institutional responsibility.

Today there is broad acceptance that Rodney did not accidentally kill himself and that Gregory Smith was responsible for delivering the disguised explosive device. The principal unanswered questions concern the extent of official planning, who authorised the operation, which state officials knowingly participated in the cover-up, and why those questions were never resolved through criminal justice rather than historical inquiry.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://parliament.gov.gy/documents/documents-laid/5851-report_of_the_commission_of_inquiry_appointed_to_enquire_and_report_on_the_circumstances_surrounding_the_death_in_an_explosion_of_the_late_dr_walter_rodney.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: parliament.gov.gy">[parliament.gov.gy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">parliament.gov.gy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">REPORTOFTHE COMMISSION OF INQUIRYAPPOINTED TO ENQUIRE AND REPORT ON THE CIRCUMSTANCESMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

For Guyana, the case has become more than the story of one political killing. It serves as a continuing test of how democratic societies confront periods of political violence, preserve historical evidence and distinguish between historical truth established through inquiry and accountability established in court. That unresolved gap explains why Walter Rodney's assassination continues to occupy a unique place among Guyana's enduring historical controversies.
