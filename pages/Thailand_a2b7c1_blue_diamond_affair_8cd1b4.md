## Introduction

The Blue Diamond Affair is one of [Thailand]({{ 'which-mysteries-still-haunt-thailands/' | relative_url }})'s most notorious unresolved historical scandals because a straightforward theft rapidly became something far darker. What began in 1989 with the theft of priceless jewellery from the Saudi royal family evolved into allegations of police corruption, the murders of Saudi diplomats, the disappearance of a Saudi businessman, disputed evidence and a diplomatic rupture that lasted for more than three decades. The central mystery is no longer simply the fate of the missing blue diamond. It is why an investigation that initially appeared successful collapsed into conflicting claims, missing evidence and crimes that have never been convincingly solved. Although Thailand and Saudi Arabia restored full diplomatic relations in 2022, the core questions about the missing jewel and the associated killings remain unanswered.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/1/26/saudi-restores-full-ties-with-thailand-after-gem-theft-dispute" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aljazeera.com">[Al Jazeera]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aljazeera.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Al JazeeraSaudi Arabia restores ties with Thailand after gem theft dispute &#124; News &#124; Al JazeeraJanuary 26, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 26, 2022</span></span></span>

## The jewel theft and disputed recovery

The affair began in 1989 when Kriangkrai Techamong, a Thai worker employed in the palace of Saudi Prince Faisal bin Fahd in Riyadh, stole a large quantity of jewellery estimated to be worth around US$20 million. Among the reported items was a rare blue diamond that later became the symbol of the entire case. Kriangkrai concealed the jewels among his belongings and shipped them back to Thailand before returning home himself. He was eventually arrested after Saudi authorities alerted Thai police, and he confessed to the theft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-global/stolen-jewels-several-murders-missing-diamond-affair-saudi-arabia-thailand-8281351/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: indianexpress.com">[The Indian Express]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">indianexpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Indian ExpressStolen jewels, several murders, and a missing diamond: What soured Saudi-Thai ties 30 years ago &#124; Explained News - The...</span></span></span>

At first, the investigation appeared remarkably successful. Thai police recovered many jewels from Kriangkrai and from dealers who had purchased items from him. Yet the apparent success soon collapsed.

When Thai officials formally returned the recovered property to Saudi Arabia, Saudi experts concluded that many valuable pieces were missing. Even more damaging, numerous jewels in the returned collection appeared to be substitutes rather than the originals. The most famous missing item, the blue diamond itself, was not among the recovered property.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/1/26/saudi-restores-full-ties-with-thailand-after-gem-theft-dispute" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aljazeera.com">[Al Jazeera]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aljazeera.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Al JazeeraSaudi Arabia restores ties with Thailand after gem theft dispute &#124; News &#124; Al JazeeraJanuary 26, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 26, 2022</span></span></span>

This transformed an ordinary criminal investigation into a question of institutional integrity. Two competing explanations emerged:

* Some observers argued that the missing jewels had been dispersed before police recovered them.
* Others believed members of the investigation or influential figures diverted valuable stones and replaced them with imitations before the official handover.

Despite decades of speculation, no court has established who, if anyone, substituted the jewels, and the blue diamond has never been recovered. Rumours that prominent Thai figures possessed the missing gems have circulated for years but have never been supported by publicly verified evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thediplomat.com/2022/01/thai-prime-minister-arrives-in-saudi-arabia-easing-diamond-heist-row/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thediplomat.com">[The Diplomat]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thediplomat.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on thediplomat.com.</span></span></span>

## Why the investigation became a national scandal

The Blue Diamond Affair became far more than an unsolved theft because confidence in the investigation steadily eroded.

The investigation was led by senior police officers who initially received praise for recovering much of the stolen property. As inconsistencies emerged over the returned jewels, suspicion increasingly shifted towards the investigators themselves rather than the original thief.

The affair exposed several weaknesses that repeatedly appear in controversial historical investigations:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* uncertain chains of custody for crucial evidence;
* allegations of corruption inside law enforcement;
* conflicting official accounts;
* long delays before prosecutions;
* investigations that failed to restore public confidence even when formal legal processes concluded.

</div>

Rather than producing a definitive explanation, each new investigation seemed to generate further questions. This gradual collapse of credibility is one reason the Blue Diamond Affair remains part of Thailand's historical memory long after the original theft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thediplomat.com/2022/01/thai-prime-minister-arrives-in-saudi-arabia-easing-diamond-heist-row/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thediplomat.com">[The Diplomat]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thediplomat.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on thediplomat.com.</span></span></span>

## The killings, disappearance and police crimes

The greatest mystery is not the missing jewel but the violence that followed.

### The murdered Saudi diplomats

In 1989 and early 1990, four Saudi diplomats were killed in Bangkok in separate attacks. Three were murdered on a single night in February 1990. The killings have never been solved.

Saudi Arabia long believed the murders were connected to efforts to recover the missing jewellery or to prevent further investigation. Thai authorities never established a legally proven connection between the diplomatic killings and the theft itself. As a result, historians distinguish between what is documented—the murders occurred—and what remains unproved—the motive behind them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Diamond_Affair" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blue Diamond Affair</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Diamond Affair</span></span></span>

### The disappearance of Mohammad al-Ruwaili

Soon afterwards, Saudi businessman Mohammad al-Ruwaili travelled to Bangkok to investigate the missing jewels independently. He disappeared in February 1990 and has never been found.

Years later, several Thai police officers were prosecuted over his presumed murder. However, in 2014 the Criminal Court dismissed the case, finding insufficient evidence for conviction. Legally, the disappearance therefore remains unresolved even though suspicion has long centred on police involvement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/article/1461905/thai-officers-cleared-killing-saudi-blue-diamond-affair" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: scmp.com">[South China Morning Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">scmp.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">South China Morning PostThai officers cleared of killing in Saudi ‘blue diamond’ affair &#124; South China Morning PostMarch 31, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 31, 2014</span></span></span>

### Crimes committed by investigators themselves

The affair acquired another disturbing dimension when Lieutenant General Chalor Kerdthes, the senior police officer celebrated for recovering the stolen jewellery, was later convicted in an entirely different murder case.

He was found guilty of ordering the kidnapping and murder of the wife and son of a Bangkok gem dealer in 1995, reportedly in an attempt to extort information connected with the jewels. Although legally separate from the diplomatic murders, the conviction reinforced public perceptions that the Blue Diamond investigation had become entangled with organised corruption and criminal violence at senior levels of law enforcement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Diamond_Affair" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blue Diamond Affair</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Diamond Affair</span></span></span>

## Rumours, diplomacy and what remains unproved

The affair has generated countless rumours, but careful distinctions remain essential.

The following points are well established:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* jewellery was stolen from the Saudi royal household;
* Kriangkrai confessed to the theft;
* much of the returned jewellery was disputed;
* the blue diamond has never been recovered;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Diamond_Affair" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blue Diamond Affair</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Diamond Affair</span></span></span>
* four Saudi diplomats were murdered;
* Mohammad al-Ruwaili disappeared and is presumed dead;
* diplomatic relations between Saudi Arabia and Thailand deteriorated for more than thirty years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/1/26/saudi-restores-full-ties-with-thailand-after-gem-theft-dispute" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aljazeera.com">[aljazeera.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aljazeera.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Al JazeeraSaudi Arabia restores ties with Thailand after gem theft dispute &#124; News &#124; Al JazeeraJanuary 26, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 26, 2022</span></span></span>

</div>

By contrast, several widely repeated claims remain unproved:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* that senior Thai political figures secretly possessed the missing blue diamond;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Diamond_Affair" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blue Diamond Affair</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Diamond Affair</span></span></span>
* that all the murders formed part of a single coordinated conspiracy;
* that investigators conclusively identified who replaced genuine jewels with fake ones;
* that the blue diamond still exists in a known private collection.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Diamond_Affair" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blue Diamond Affair</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Diamond Affair</span></span></span>

</div>

These ideas continue to circulate because the official investigations left significant gaps, not because convincing evidence has emerged to confirm them. Responsible historical analysis therefore separates persistent rumour from established fact.

One additional uncertainty concerns the diamond itself. Although Saudi officials consistently maintained that an exceptionally valuable blue diamond was among the stolen jewels, later commentators have noted the scarcity of publicly available photographs or detailed descriptions. That absence has encouraged speculation, but it does not invalidate the documented theft or the diplomatic dispute that followed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Diamond_Affair" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blue Diamond Affair</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Diamond Affair</span></span></span>

## Why the case still matters

The Blue Diamond Affair remains historically significant because it demonstrated how failures in criminal investigation can reshape international relations.

Saudi Arabia downgraded diplomatic relations with Thailand, restricted labour opportunities for Thai workers, reduced official contacts and discouraged travel for decades. The dispute carried substantial economic and political costs well beyond the original theft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.euronews.com/2022/01/24/us-saudi-thailand" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euronews.com">[euronews]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euronews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Thai PM to visit Saudi Arabia as diplomatic relations thaw &#124; Euronews</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Thai PM to visit Saudi Arabia as diplomatic relations thaw &#124; Euronews...</span></span></span>

Only in January 2022 did the two governments announce the restoration of full diplomatic relations and the exchange of ambassadors after more than thirty years of estrangement. The diplomatic thaw ended one chapter of the affair but did not resolve its central mysteries. The blue diamond has never been found, the murders of the diplomats remain unsolved, and the disappearance of Mohammad al-Ruwaili has never been satisfactorily explained.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/1/26/saudi-restores-full-ties-with-thailand-after-gem-theft-dispute" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aljazeera.com">[Al Jazeera]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aljazeera.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Al JazeeraSaudi Arabia restores ties with Thailand after gem theft dispute &#124; News &#124; Al JazeeraJanuary 26, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 26, 2022</span></span></span>

For historians, the Blue Diamond Affair stands as a cautionary example of how an apparently simple theft can evolve into a lasting historical mystery when investigations lose public credibility. Its enduring puzzle lies not merely in a missing gemstone but in the wider failure to establish an authoritative account of the crimes that followed, leaving decades of rumour, suspicion and unanswered questions woven into the history of modern Thailand.
